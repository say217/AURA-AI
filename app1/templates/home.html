What is AURA AI

Built for diagnostics, powered by deep learning

A modular Flask-based platform leveraging PyTorch ResNet architectures and Grad-CAM explainability to support rapid, transparent assessment of histopathology and radiology images across three cancer families.

Multi-modal: CT, X-Ray, Mammography, Histopathology
Custom Grad-CAM with high-resolution color-coded heatmaps

Core Capabilities

Advanced AI-powered diagnostic features

Comprehensive tools engineered to enhance radiological workflows and accelerate time-to-treatment across multiple cancer types.

Breast Cancer — IDC Detection

Binary classification (IDC+/IDC−) on histopathology patches. Grad-CAM heatmaps highlight tissue regions driving the prediction with batch upload support.

Lung & Colon — 5-Class

Differentiates Lung Adenocarcinoma, Squamous Cell Carcinoma, Benign Lung, Colon Adenocarcinoma, and Benign Colon with probability bar charts per class.

Brain Cancer — ResNet-101

Deep residual network (101 layers) for high-stakes brain tumor classification from MRI scans, with Grad-CAM overlay for precise localized explainability.

Explainable AI — Grad-CAM

Custom-built from scratch — no black-box wrappers. Color-coded saliency maps superimposed on original images so every clinical decision is transparent.

Comprehensive Reports

Structured diagnostic reports with annotated heatmaps, probability breakdowns, severity grading, and clinical interpretation text per prediction.

Secure Data Pipeline

HIPAA-compliant, bcrypt-authenticated, session-based access with env-variable config, modular Blueprint architecture, and Gunicorn + Nginx readiness.

Batch Image Processing

Drag-and-drop or multi-select histopathology slides (.png .jpg .jpeg .tif). Real-time batch inference with individual Grad-CAM results returned instantly.

Historical Comparison

Longitudinal tracking of lesion changes across patient sessions. Automated delta detection enables precise monitoring of disease progression over time.

Quality Assurance

Automated image quality scoring flags artifacts and suboptimal exposure before inference — ensuring every prediction is based on reliable input data.
